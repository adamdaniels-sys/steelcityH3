Mark this hash as done?

Hang on — this hash is . It hasn't happened yet. You almost certainly don't want to do this. If you were just trying to save your work, close this and use Save instead.

Marking it done will:

  • Close RSVPs — nobody can say they're coming any more.
  • Move it off "Coming up" on the homepage and into Past Hashes.
  • Lock in the money and the attendance as the final record.

You can undo this afterwards with "Re-open this hash", so it's not the end of the world if it's wrong.

Re-open this hash?

Nothing you've typed is lost. The register, who paid, the write-up, the photos and the hares all stay exactly as you left them, and you can carry on editing any of it.

What changes while it's re-opened:

  • It shows under "Coming up" again, and people can RSVP.
  • Anyone who still owes money for this hash stops showing as owing it — until you mark it done again.
  • The charity total on the homepage drops by this hash's share, and comes back when you finish it again.

Both of those come straight back the moment you mark it done again. Nothing is deleted at any point.

Delete this event?

All RSVPs and attendance for this event will be wiped too. Cannot be undone.
